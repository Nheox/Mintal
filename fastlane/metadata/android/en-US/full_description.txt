Mintal is a focus timer built around real plants of the genus <i>Mentha</i>.

Choose how long you want to concentrate. While the session runs, a single progress bar
fills and nothing else asks for your attention. Finish the block without leaving the app
and the session is planted in <b>the Grove</b>: one continuously growing bed of mints that
is the record of your attention, not a reward animation bolted on top of it.

One finished session is one node — a stem segment and the pair of leaves on top of it — so
a plant grows the way mint actually grows, pair by opposite pair, branching from its leaf
axils. When a plant is full grown it stops, and your next session of that species starts
another beside it.

Every plant is a real member of the genus: spearmint, peppermint, water mint, pennyroyal,
Corsican mint and more, each drawn from a photographic reference down to leaf shape,
margin, surface and hairs. Cumulative focused time earns new species, and <i>which</i> one
you unlock at each milestone is your choice.

<b>Features</b>

* Countdown and count-up sessions, kept alive by a foreground service
* Honest blocking: leaving mid-session gets a visible 15 second warning first, never a
  silent failure, and never control of your device
* An optional allowlist, so a dictionary or a calculator does not cost you a session
* An optional floating bubble with the time left, over whatever app is in front
* Tags, ratings and notes on finished sessions
* Statistics with hand-drawn charts: focus by hour, by day, by tag
* CSV export and a full JSON backup you can restore
* Light and dark themes

<b>Privacy</b>

Mintal's manifest contains <b>no INTERNET permission</b>, so the app cannot send your data
anywhere even by accident — you can verify that in the source. There is no account, no
server, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, no Google Play
Services and nothing for sale. Everything lives in a database on your device and works in
airplane mode.
